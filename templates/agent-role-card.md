# Agent Role Card

> Every agent working in the product organization gets a role card, the same way every person has a role. It answers the questions a new team member would ask: what does this agent do, who does it work for, what may it decide, and how will we know it is doing well?
>
> This is the one-page version for the team. The full technical spec lives in [`agent-workflows`](https://github.com/utaknablein/agent-workflows/blob/main/agent-spec-template.md).

---

**Agent:** *Name*
**Role in one sentence:** *What work does it do, and which decision does that work improve?*
**Accountable owner:** *One named person. Not a team.*
**Works with:** *The people and agents it hands work to and receives work from*
**Status:** ☐ Pilot  ☐ Live  ☐ Under review  ☐ Retired

---

## Responsibilities

| This agent... | For this decision or work |
|---|---|
| **Prepares** | *e.g. the first draft of every decision memo for stalled threads* |
| **Checks** | *e.g. new plans against the roadmap for conflicts* |
| **Never decides** | *e.g. priorities, owners, anything sent outside the team* |

## Decision rights

| May do alone | Needs a person's approval | Must never do |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

> Write the "never" column first. It is the column that protects the team, the customer and the company.

## Hand-offs

*Who receives its output, in what form, and where a person reviews it before anything happens.*

## How we review it

Quarterly, by the accountable owner, like any team member:

- Is its work accepted without substantial rework?
- Does it catch things the team would have missed?
- Has it made an error, or invented a source?
- Does it escalate when it should?
- **Are the decisions it supports faster or better?** (Check the ["Prepared by" column](../decision-velocity/decision-log.md) in the decision log.)

Verdict: ☐ Keep  ☐ Fix  ☐ Retire

## What the team should know

*Anything people working alongside it need to know: its known blind spots, what it cannot see, and how to flag a problem with its work.*
