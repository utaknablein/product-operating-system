# Decision Memo

> Use this when a decision needs more than a hallway conversation. Keep it to one page. If it's longer, you probably have two decisions in one memo.

---

**Decision:** *One sentence, phrased as a question with a yes/no or A/B answer.*
*Example: Do we sunset the legacy recommendations service before Q3?*

**Owner:** *One name. The person who decides, not the person who writes the memo.*

**Decide by:** *A date. A decision without a date is a discussion.*

**Type:** ☐ One-way door (hard to reverse)  ☐ Two-way door (easy to reverse)

> Two-way doors should be decided fast and by the person closest to the work. Spend your care on one-way doors.

---

## Why now

*What happens if we don't decide by the date above? Be specific: cost, risk, a window closing.*

## Options

| | Option | What it gets us | What it costs | What it rules out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | | | | |
| B | | | | |
| C | *Do nothing* | | | |

> Always include "do nothing." It's the option most orgs actually choose, just without saying so.

## Recommendation

*Which option and why, in three sentences or fewer.*

## What would change our mind

*The one or two facts that, if they turned out differently, would make us choose another option. This is the most useful section in the memo. It tells everyone what to watch after the decision is made.*

## What this stops

*If we choose the recommendation, what do we stop doing or not start? (See [Subtract before you add](../principles.md#4-subtract-before-you-add).)*

## Who was consulted

*Names and one line each on where they landed. Disagreement is fine and should be visible. "Disagree and commit" only works if the disagreement was recorded.*

---

## Decision record

*Filled in after the decision.*

- **Decided:** *Option, date*
- **Decided by:** *Name*
- **Days from memo to decision:** *Log this in the [decision log](../decision-velocity/decision-log.md)*
- **Review on:** *Date to check whether "what would change our mind" happened*
